# Overview, V12 changes

V12 revises V11. It makes every Board private (Decision Y), renames the V10 share link to the Public_Link and restricts it to Manager and Admin (Decision Z), adds an Invite_Link with Join_Requests that only a Manager or an Admin approves (Decision AA), replaces the Card modal draft model with Auto_Save (Decision AB), adds comment deletion and an Attachment removal modal (Decision AC), states the Member Subtask matrix and derives Card progress from Subtasks (Decision AD), makes Inbox rows links and adds three Notification types (Decision AE), and gives the Profile page a two-column layout (Decision AF). V11 branding (Decision X, Requirement 21) is unchanged. Everything in `specs/v10/` and `specs/v11/` that this version does not name stands.

Local development data only: the V12 migration drops `boards.visibility`, adds `boards.invite_token` and `board_join_requests`, alters `notifications`, and resets the local database (it aborts unless `APP_ENV` is `development` or `testing`); nothing is backfilled, and `flask seed-data` recreates the data.

## Glossary (added or replaced)

- **Guest**: A visitor without an account or Token. Can open a Board only through a Public_Link (Decisions K and Z) and sees its columns and cards read-only, never its comments or activity. A Guest cannot list or browse Boards, and can open an Invite_Link page only after signing in (Decision AA).
- **Board**: A project container holding Columns, Cards, and Categories. Every Board is private (Decision Y): it has no `visibility`. It has a nullable `owner_id`, a nullable `share_token` (the Public_Link), and a nullable `invite_token` (the Invite_Link).
- **Share_Token**: The random, unguessable, unique string stored in `Board.share_token` (at least 256 bits from a cryptographically secure source). A null value means the Public_Link is off. The column and the name are unchanged in V12 (Decisions K and Z).
- **Public_Link**: The URL `/shared/{token}` built from a Board's Share_Token (the V10 Share_Link, renamed in V12; "Share_Link" in older text means the same thing). Opens the Shared_View for anyone, signed in or not, and is created, regenerated, and disabled only by a Manager or an Admin (Decision Z).
- **Shared_View**: The read-only Frontend view at `/shared/{token}` of a Board's Columns, Cards, Card details, Categories, and Attachment links, without Comments, Activity, Inbox, or any write control, shown to every visitor of a Public_Link who does not already have Board_Access (Decisions K and Z).
- **Board_Owner**: The Supervisor referenced by a Board's `owner_id`. Sees the Board, edits it as a Supervisor with Board_Staff_Access, manages the Invite_Link, and sees pending Join_Requests but does not decide them (Decisions Y and AA).
- **Board_Member**: A join record `(board_id, user_id)` (stored as `BoardMember`), unique per pair, that attaches a Member or a Supervisor to a Board beyond the Board's `owner_id`. A Manager or an Admin is never a Board_Member (Decision Y). Created by a Manager or an Admin directly, or by an approved Join_Request.
- **Board_Access**: A signed-in user has Board_Access to a non-soft-deleted Board exactly when the user is its Board_Owner, a Board_Member, a Manager, or an Admin (Decision Y). Every other signed-in user gets HTTP 403 and a Guest gets HTTP 401. A Public_Link grants only the Shared_View of Requirement 23, and an Invite_Link grants nothing but the right to ask. Card, Comment, Attachment, Activity_Log, and Category access resolves through the owning Board's Board_Access.
- **Board_Staff_Access**: Write access to a Board's content. A user has Board_Staff_Access when the user is a Manager, an Admin, or a Supervisor who is the Board's Board_Owner or a Board_Member. A Member never has it; a Member's writes are limited to the Assigned_Member rules (Decision 2).
- **Invite_Link**: The URL `/invite/{token}` built from a Board's `invite_token`. It lets a signed-in Member or Supervisor send a Join_Request and grants no access by itself. Generated, regenerated, and disabled by the Board_Owner, a Manager, or an Admin (Decision AA).
- **Join_Request**: A `BoardJoinRequest` row `(id, board_id, user_id, status, decided_by, created_at, updated_at)` with status `pending`, `approved`, or `rejected`. At most one pending row exists per `(board_id, user_id)`. Only a Manager or an Admin decides it (Decision AA).
- **Auto_Save**: The Card modal behaviour of Decision AB: each field, Subtask, and Attachment change is sent by its own request when the user commits it, with a visible Saving, Saved, or error state per field, and no Save changes button.
- **Derived_Progress**: The Card's `progress` integer from 0 to 10 computed from its Subtasks (`done` of `total`), null when there are none, never stored or edited (Decision AD).
- **Notification**: A server-created record targeting one user, of type `assigned`, `deadline`, `board_join_requested`, `board_added`, or `board_join_rejected` (Decision AE).
- **Inbox**: The side panel listing the signed-in user's Notifications. Each row is a link to the Card or Board it is about (Decision AE).
- **App_Header**: The persistent top bar of every Protected_Route page: the Sidebar toggle below 960 pixels, the page context (on a Board, the name, the "Public link" button for a Manager or an Admin, and the "Invite" button for the Board_Owner, a Manager, or an Admin; there is no visibility icon), the Inbox bell, and the User_Menu trigger (Decision L).
- **Protected_Route**: A Frontend page that requires a Session_Cookie and redirects a visitor without one to `/login`. Every page under the `(app)` route group is one: `/boards`, `/boards/{id}` and its sub-pages, `/profile`, `/admin/users`, and `/invite/{token}` (Decision P, resolving Open Question 5; `/invite/{token}` added in V12, Decision AA). `/login`, `/register`, and `/shared/{token}` are not.
- **Safe_Return_Path**: The value of the `next` query parameter on `/login` when it is a path and query that starts with a single `/`, does not start with `//` or `/\`, has no control character, has at most 512 characters, and starts with `/boards`, `/profile`, `/admin`, or `/invite/` (V12, Decision AA); any other value is ignored (Decision T).
- **Test_Account**: A seeded, existing user whose Username or Email and password are supplied to the E2E_Auth_Suite through the environment variables `E2E_USER_IDENTIFIER` and `E2E_USER_PASSWORD` (the variable names are unchanged from V9). The suite assumes a Member account and never creates, edits, or deletes accounts (V9). The suite makes at most one failed login attempt against this account per run (AUTH-E2E-SI-06), far below the lockout threshold, and each successful sign-in resets the pair counter. (V12: the Test_Account `member` is a Board_Member of a seeded Board that has a Card assigned to it, so the V12 criteria 69 and 70 have something to open; the suite still never creates or changes data.)

Removed terms: Public Board, Private Board, Board visibility, and Share_Link as a separate term (now the Public_Link).

## Open Questions (changed)

The members endpoint returns the owner with `is_owner`; the member search never lists the owner (ACC-09); the assignee popover lists the owner first and lets staff assign them (Requirement 4 criterion 33); tests are Requirement 15 criteria 36 and 37.
24. **Join_Request spam and Email for approvers (V12, RESOLVED by the owner in round 2 within V12):** (a) no cooldown after a rejection, and no rate limit on Join_Request creation (Requirement 24 criterion 12 stands); (b) the pending join-request list shows each requester's Email together with the full name and the Avatar_Image or Initials_Avatar, to the Board_Owner, a Manager, and an Admin only. This reverses the earlier "no Email in the pending list" choice and amends Decisions M and S: the Email now has three staff-only places (candidates, assignee popover, pending join-request list). Requirement 24 criteria 13, 19, and 27; Requirement 3 criterion 31; Requirement 15 criteria 36 and 37.

No requirement changes. The owner can reverse this by asking for a `comment.created` action.
26. **Role change cleanup (V12, RESOLVED BY DEFAULT in round 2 within V12; the owner did not answer, so the recommendation written here applies):** Requirement 22 criterion 6 deletes memberships and pending requests when an Admin makes a user a Manager or an Admin. Demoting a Manager to Member leaves them with no Board access until a Manager or an Admin adds them or a Join_Request is approved; this is accepted as a rare case. No requirement changes.
27. **Rejected requester destination (V12, RESOLVED BY DEFAULT in round 2 within V12; the owner did not answer, so the decision already written applies):** the brief said board notifications open `/boards/{id}`; for `board_join_rejected` the requester has no access, so the row opens `/boards` instead (Requirement 28, criterion 5). The owner can reverse this.
28. **Old Share_Link links (V12, resolved by Decision Z; unchanged in round 2 within V12):** the owner of a Board who enabled a link in V10 loses the right to manage it (Decision Z); the data was reset, so no existing link is affected.
