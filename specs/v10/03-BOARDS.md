### Requirement 3: Boards and Columns

**User Story:** As a Manager or Admin, I want to create boards and assign them to supervisors, so that each project has an owner and a consistent workflow.

#### Acceptance Criteria
**Round 3 within V10 (Decisions U to W):** criteria 24, 25, 30, and 32 reworded (avatars in member lists and candidates; candidate search needs at least 2 characters).

V10 changes: criteria 23 and 25 reworded (App_Header and Share button; candidate search by Username prefix and exact Email). All other criteria are unchanged from V8. **Round 2 within V10:** criterion 25 reworded (the candidate response and the match on full name, Username prefix, and exact Email; Email returned to staff, Decision S); criteria 30 to 32 added (members list `include=email`, where the Email is shown, the member search row).

23. THE Frontend SHALL show the Board header in two rows: the first is the App_Header (Requirement 19) and holds the visibility icon and Board name and, for the Board_Owner, a Manager, or an Admin, the Share button (Requirement 18) on the left, and the Inbox bell and the User_Menu trigger on the right (reworded in V10); the second holds three bordered sections, progress ("12 of 18 done", the percent, and a thin bar) on the left, the Category filter chips in the middle as one strip that scrolls sideways with left and right arrow buttons shown only when more chips are hidden, and the search field on the right; the header SHALL have no Add Member button and no settings icon, and below 960 pixels the search field SHALL collapse to an icon that opens the search in a modal (Decision I).
24. THE Frontend SHALL keep one Team members section in the Sidebar, listing the Board's members, each with an Avatar_Image or Initials_Avatar (Decision U), with no separate Members navigation entry; WHERE the viewer is the Board_Owner, a Manager, or an Admin, the section heading SHALL carry an add button that opens a member search (a popover, or a modal when the viewport is too narrow) and each member SHALL carry a remove button that asks for confirmation (Decision I).
25. WHEN the Board_Owner, a Manager, or an Admin requests `GET /api/boards/{id}/candidates?search=<text>` with a `search` of at least 2 characters after trimming, THE Backend SHALL return at most 20 users who are not yet BoardMembers of that Board, matched case-insensitively on `full_name`, on `username` by prefix, or on `email` by exact match only (never by prefix or substring), and ordered by name, each with `id`, `full_name`, `username`, `email`, and `avatar_url` (Decisions M, I, S, U, and W; reworded in V10 and again in rounds 2 and 3); IF `search` is missing or has fewer than 2 characters after trimming, THEN THE Backend SHALL return HTTP 400 with the code `VALIDATION_ERROR` and an `error.details` entry for `search` and SHALL query no users; IF another signed-in user calls it, THEN THE Backend SHALL return HTTP 403, and with no Token HTTP 401 (Decision I).

30. [EML-01] WHEN a signed-in user requests `GET /api/boards/{id}/members` without `include`, THE Backend SHALL return each member with `id`, `full_name`, `username`, and `avatar_url` and no `email`; WHEN the request carries `include=email` and the viewer has Board_Staff_Access, THE Backend SHALL add `email` to each member; IF the request carries `include=email` and the viewer lacks Board_Staff_Access, THEN THE Backend SHALL return HTTP 403, and with no Token HTTP 401 (Decision S).
31. [EML-02] THE Frontend SHALL show a user's Email only in the candidate results of the member search (Board_Owner, Manager, Admin) and in the assignee popover (Board_Staff_Access), and THE Backend SHALL return another user's Email in no other Board-scoped payload, including Comments, Activity_Log rows, Notifications, the Shared_View, the Sidebar member rows, and any list a Member can read (Decision S). A user's own Email in the `user` shape and the Admin user list are unchanged.
32. [EML-03] WHEN the member search shows a candidate, THE Frontend SHALL show the Avatar_Image or Initials_Avatar, the full name and, beneath it in muted text, the Username and the Email, SHALL keep matching on full name, Username prefix, and exact Email, and SHALL send no search request until the trimmed text has 2 characters, showing the hint "Type at least 2 characters." meanwhile (Decisions S, U, and W).
